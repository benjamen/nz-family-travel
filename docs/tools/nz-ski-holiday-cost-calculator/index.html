Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a family ski trip cost in NZ?
For a family of 4 (2 adults, 2 children) skiing 3 days at Coronet Peak or The Remarkables with standard gear hire, ski school for the kids, and budget accommodation, expect roughly $2,500–$3,500 for the trip excluding flights. Lift passes alone run $450–$550/day for a family of 4. Use the calculator above for a personalised estimate based on your field, group size and days.
Which NZ ski field is cheapest for families?
Whakapapa on Mt Ruapehu (North Island) has the lowest lift pass prices — adult day passes from $95 versus $180 at Coronet Peak/The Remarkables and $184+ at Cardrona. Accommodation around Ruapehu (National Park, Ohakune) is also generally cheaper than Queenstown or Wanaka in peak season.
Is it cheaper to buy NZ ski lift passes online in advance?
Yes. Most NZ fields — including Coronet Peak, The Remarkables and Cardrona — offer a discount of roughly 10-15% for lift passes bought online ahead of your visit compared with walk-up ticket window prices.
How much is ski gear hire in Queenstown or Wanaka?
A standard adult ski or snowboard package (skis or board, boots, poles) typically costs around $40/day, with premium/advanced packages around $65/day. Children's packages are typically around $25/day. Hiring on-mountain at the field is usually more convenient than hiring in town.
